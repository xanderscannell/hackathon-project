## How much of Michigan gets rated

| Fact | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Michigan has about 165,000 lane miles of non-federal-aid roads. "These road agencies are not required to collect PASER data on these types of roads." TAMC had data for 9,239 lane miles of them in 2017, "which is only about 6 percent of the total." | [Mackinac Center, Roads in Michigan, Oct 2, 2018](https://www.mackinac.org/S2018-10) | Fetched. 2017 data; say "about 6% (2017)" |

## What bad roads cost

All from [TRIP, Keeping Michigan Mobile, August 2025](https://tripnet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/TRIP_Keeping_Michigan_Mobile_Report_August_2025.pdf) (fetched, text read from the PDF):

| Fact | Quote |
|---|---|
| Cost per driver | Rough roads cost the average Michigan driver $772 a year in additional vehicle operating costs, $6 billion statewide (paraphrased). |
| Detroit area | Detroit drivers lose $2,921 a year in total ($1,015 of it vehicle operating costs). 42% of the Detroit area's major roads are poor. |
| Statewide condition | "40 percent of major local and state-maintained roads in Michigan are in poor or mediocre condition." |
| Preventive vs rebuild | A 2023 Public Sector Consultants report "noted that construction of transportation assets is five to eight times more expensive per lane mile than preventative maintenance." |
| Deferral | "every $1 of deferred maintenance on roads and bridges costs an additional $4 to $5 in needed future repairs." |
| Rebuild vs resurface | "reconstructing roads costs approximately four times more than resurfacing them." |
