Every fleet vehicle becomes a road inspector.

The problem

30
DAYS

Michigan law: once a road defect has been readily apparent for 30 days, the agency is conclusively presumed to know about it.

  • Federal-aid roads get a PASER rating once every two years.
  • Local roads don't have to be rated at all: about 6% of their lane miles had PASER data in 2017.
  • Rough roads cost the average Michigan driver $772 a year in vehicle costs.

MCL 691.1403. City of Rochester, PASER information. Mackinac Center, 2018 (TAMC data). TRIP, Keeping Michigan Mobile, Aug 2025.

The idea

Fleet vehicles already drive every road, every week.
Make those trips measure the road.

Measure

Roughness over every 50 m of road, and every severe hit, matched to the PASER segment it was on.

Confirm

A hit becomes a pothole once it's found on two or more passes. One jolt could be a manhole cover.

Act

A 30-day clock per pothole, a work order in one click, and the fair roads to seal before they turn poor.

The box

  • ESP32, BNO085 motion sensor, GT-U7 GPS and a microSD card.
  • Finds roughness and severe hits on the board.
  • Keeps everything on the SD card, uploads over WiFi. Nothing is lost offline.
  • About $100 in parts, estimated at single-unit retail prices.

Michigan impact

Built for Michigan's road agencies.

Who buys it

Michigan's 83 county road agencies, which maintain 90,484 miles, 75% of the state's roads, and its cities. Priced per vehicle per year.

Who rides

Boxes go on trucks the agency already runs. Coverage grows with every vehicle, not with staff. About $100 in parts each.

Michigan jobs

Boxes assembled in Michigan. Calibration by Michigan's PASER raters, trained by Michigan Tech's Center for Technology and Training.

Money it saves

Rebuilding a road costs 5 to 8 times more per lane mile than preventive work. Knowing which fair roads are slipping is how a fixed budget goes further.

County Road Association of Michigan. Michigan Tech CTT. Public Sector Consultants 2023, via TRIP, Aug 2025. Parts cost: estimated from single-unit retail prices.
